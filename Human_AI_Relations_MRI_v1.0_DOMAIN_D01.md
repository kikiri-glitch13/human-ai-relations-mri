### `HAI-SRC-0012` — Risko & Gilbert (2016)

Risko, E. F., & Gilbert, S. J. (2016). Cognitive Offloading. Trends in Cognitive Sciences, 20(9), 676–688.

- Evidence layer: `Layer A`
- Supports: External ToolによるCognitive Offloadingが確立された現象であること。
- Does Not Support: Generative AI-specific Deskilling。
- Key limitation: Generative AI以前の研究伝統。
- Local source ID: `D1-S09`

### `HAI-SRC-0013` — Barnett & Ceci (2002)

Barnett, S. M., & Ceci, S. J. (2002). When and where do we apply what we learn? A taxonomy for far transfer. Psychological Bulletin, 128(4), 612–637.

- Evidence layer: `Layer A — Existing Research Tradition`
- Supports: Transferが単一Outcomeではなく、Content、Context、Time、Modality等によって異なること。
- Does Not Support: Generative AI固有のTransfer Effect。
- Key limitation: AI-specific Evidenceではない。
- Local source ID: `D1-S01`

### `HAI-SRC-0014` — Roediger & Karpicke (2006)

Roediger, H. L., III, & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention. Psychological Science, 17(3), 249–255.

- Evidence layer: `Layer A`
- Supports: Immediate PerformanceとDelayed Retentionが異なる方向へ動き得ること。Retrieval PracticeがDelayed Retentionを改善し得ること。
- Does Not Support: AI use necessarily reduces Retrieval Practice or Retention.
- Local source ID: `D1-S02`

### `HAI-SRC-0015` — Bastani et al. (2025)

Bastani, H., Bastani, O., Sungu, A., Ge, H., Kabakcı, Ö., & Mariman, R. (2025). Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 122(26), e2422633122.

- Evidence layer: `Layer B — Direct AI-Specific Evidence`
- Evidence status: `B within studied conditions`
- Supports: Assisted Performanceとshort-term AI-free Performanceの乖離。System Design matters.
- Does Not Support: Delayed Retention Loss、Long-term Deskilling、Generative AI一般の教育的有害性。
- Key limitation: Same-session / short-term outcomeであり、Delayed Retentionではない。
- Local source ID: `D1-S03`

### `HAI-SRC-0016` — Kestin et al. (2025)

Kestin, G., Miller, K., Klales, A., Milbourne, T., & Ponti, G. (2025). AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting. Scientific Reports, 15, 17458.
